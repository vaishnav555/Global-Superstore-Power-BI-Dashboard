The objective of this task is to develop an interactive Retail Sales Performance Dashboard in Power BI using the Global Superstore dataset. The dataset is imported into Power BI and transformed into meaningful business insights by creating key performance indicators (KPIs), visualizations, and interactive filters.

-- First, KPI measures such as Total Sales, Total Profit, and Profit Margin are created using DAX to evaluate overall business performance. These KPIs are displayed at the top of the dashboard using card visuals to provide a quick executive summary. A sales trend line chart is then developed to analyze sales performance over time and identify seasonal or growth patterns.

-- To understand product-level performance, a category-wise bar chart is created to compare sales across different product categories. Regional performance is analyzed using a map or bar chart to identify the highest-performing regions. A Top 10 Products table is added to highlight the best-selling products contributing significantly to revenue.

-- Interactive slicers for Region, Order Date, and Category are included to allow users to dynamically filter and explore the data. Finally, an Insights text box summarizes key findings from the dashboard, and the completed report is exported to PDF for sharing with stakeholders.

-- The objective of this project is to design and develop a comprehensive Retail Sales Performance Dashboard in Power BI using the Global Superstore dataset. The task focuses on transforming raw transactional sales data into meaningful analytical insights that support business decision-making and performance monitoring.

-- The process begins by importing the Global Superstore dataset into Power BI and validating the dataset structure, ensuring that all columns such as Order Date, Sales, Profit, Category, Region, and Product Name have appropriate data types. After preparing the dataset, DAX measures are created to calculate key performance indicators (KPIs), including Total Sales, Total Profit, and Profit Margin, which provide an overall summary of the organization’s financial performance. These KPI measures are displayed at the top of the dashboard using card visuals to provide executives with an immediate overview of business performance.

-- To analyze business trends over time, a sales trend line chart is created using the Order Date field to track revenue growth and identify seasonal patterns. A category-wise bar chart is developed to compare sales performance across different product categories, helping stakeholders understand which product segments contribute most to revenue. Additionally, a region performance visualization such as a map or bar chart is included to evaluate geographical sales distribution and identify high-performing regions.

-- To highlight product-level contributions, a Top 10 Products table is created, displaying the highest-selling products based on total sales. Interactive slicers for Region, Order Date, and Category are added to allow users to dynamically filter the dashboard and explore the data from multiple perspectives. An Insights text box is also included to summarize key analytical findings derived from the dashboard, such as the highest-performing category, leading region, and major revenue-contributing products.

Finally, the completed dashboard is formatted using professional layout standards and exported as a PDF report for sharing with business stakeholders. This project demonstrates practical skills in data modeling, DAX measure creation, interactive visualization design, and business insight generation, showcasing the ability to convert raw retail data into actionable intelligence suitable for real-world business reporting.
